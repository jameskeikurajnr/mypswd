🚀Getting started

  1. Open My PSWD. The first screen shows the logo, a password box and Create new vault.
  2. Type a long password. The bar under the box shows Weak, Okay or Strong. A new vault needs a Strong password (12 or more characters).
  3. Tip: a passphrase of four or five random words is long and easy to remember.
  4. Tap Show password to check what you typed, then tap Create new vault.
  5. Set up a recovery key straight away (section 3). Without one, a forgotten password cannot be reset.

🔒Lock methods

Open the gear at the top-left, then Security, then Lock method.

MethodWhat it is
Strong password12 or more characters, checked for strength. Best protection and recommended.
Simple password6 or more characters. Quicker to type, easier to guess. You must tick that you understand the risk.
PIN4 to 6 digits. Behind the scenes the vault uses a long random key. After 10 wrong tries the PIN is removed and you use your recovery key.
Fingerprint or screen lockAndroid checks your fingerprint or your phone's screen lock, then opens the vault. The key is kept in the phone's secure Keystore.

The lock screen never says which method you chose, so someone looking over your shoulder learns nothing. Switching method creates a fresh lock, and an older recovery key stops working.

The vault locks itself after 15 minutes of no use (you can pick 1, 5, 15 or 30 in Settings, then Auto-lock) and after one minute in the background.

🗝️Recovery key

It is the spare key you keep at the bank. If you lose your normal key, the spare still opens the box.
  • It is a long code of letters and numbers, shown in groups of four.
  • Fingerprint and PIN vaults make one automatically and show it once. Strong and Simple vaults make one only when you ask: Settings, then Security, then Set up a recovery key.
  • Write it on paper and keep it somewhere safe. Do not keep it next to your backup file, because together they open everything.
  • To use it, tap Use a recovery key or master password instead on the lock screen, type the key, and continue. Dashes, spaces and capital letters do not matter.
  • Changing the lock method or master password cancels the old recovery key, so make a new one and write it down.

📒Logins and secure notes

  • On Your vault, tap the round + button, choose Login or Secure note, fill in the fields and tap Save.
  • For a password, tap Generate to make a strong random one. A slider sets the length.
  • Type a Company and matching names appear. Tap one to fill the title, category and website.
  • Pick a Category (Banking, Email, Social, University, Health, Insurance, Travel, Crypto, Developer, Cloud and more) to get smart fields such as BSB and account number.
  • Inside an item, tap + Add another login to keep several logins for one company. Give each a label such as Personal or Work. The list shows a ×2 badge when an item holds more than one login.
  • Secure notes have their own Notes tab. Tap Open on a full page for more room, and Find logins to turn a note into logins.
  • A login can show Uses a passkey as a reminder that the site also lets you sign in with a passkey.

🔎Finding and organising

  • Search by title or username at the top.
  • The buttons All, Favourites, Logins, Notes narrow the list.
  • Favourites: tick the star in an item and it is listed first.
  • Folders: type a folder name such as Work or Money. Folders have 8 colours.
  • Select: tap Select, tick items (or Select all), then use Favourite, Folder or Delete.
  • Copy: tap Copy next to a username or password. The text is cleared from the clipboard after about 20 seconds.

♻️The Bin

When you delete an item it moves to the Bin and stays there for 30 days. You can restore it or delete it for good. After 30 days it is removed automatically.

🎲Generator and one-time codes

  • Generator: random passwords, a Passphrase Maker, and normal-style passwords. You choose the type and length.
  • One-time codes (2FA): save a site's secret in a login and the app shows the changing code. It is calculated on your phone with no internet.

🛡️Safety tools

  • Health Check: scores your passwords and lists weak, reused, very common and old ones, with the most urgent first.
  • Panic Lock: hold the lock button for one second. The vault locks and the clipboard is cleared.
  • Break-in Log: shows wrong-password attempts on this phone. You can clear it.
  • Password reminders: a gentle nudge to change old passwords.
  • Wrong-password limit: optional. If you turn it on, the vault erases itself after that many wrong passwords. Use it carefully, and keep a backup.
  • Decoy vault: Settings, then Decoy vault. Pick a second password that opens a believable fake vault. Your real password still opens your real vault, and the real vault stays hidden and untouched. This stops casual or forced opening. It cannot hide from someone who studies the phone's storage with special tools.

🆘Emergency Sheet and wallet card

Make a letter or a wallet-size recovery card for someone you trust. It holds your recovery key and simple steps. Print it or write the key down, and keep it apart from your phone and your backup file. Tick Saved safely on paper when done.

💾Backups, import and moving phones

  • Back up: Settings, then the encrypted backup option. Save the .ppn file somewhere safe. It stays encrypted. Do this before every app update.
  • Automatic backups: the app also keeps a few encrypted copies on the phone. Use them to undo a mistake. They need the password that was in use at that time.
  • Backup reminder: the app nudges you when it has been a while. You can choose the number of days.
  • Import: tap Import to open a .ppn vault file. It replaces the vault on this phone, so the app asks you first. You can also import notes, Word or CSV files, with a preview before anything is added.
  • New phone: on the old phone export a .ppn backup, move the file across, install My PSWD, and import it. Keep your recovery key too.

🗄️Two vaults

Tap the card at the top of the lock screen to open your main vault, My PSWD, or add a second vault with its own password. The second vault stays private.

🎨Look and language

  • Settings has six colour themes (Gold, Mist, Ocean, Sunset, Emerald, Violet). Appearance lets you pick comfortable or compact lists, grouping, sort order and text size.
  • The app speaks English, Español, 中文(简体), हिन्दी, Français, Português and Bahasa Indonesia. Open Settings, then Language. The change is instant and your vault is never touched.

🤖What is different on Android

  • Fingerprint or screen lock is used in place of Face ID and Touch ID.
  • Face unlock is not available. Many Android phones, including Samsung, treat it as a weaker lock, and Android does not let apps use it to protect secrets.
  • AutoFill is not on Android yet. On Apple devices My PSWD can fill logins; on Android you open the app and tap Copy.
  • Screenshots are blocked inside the app on purpose, to keep your passwords safe.
  • The app needs Android 11 or newer, and has no internet permission.

🛠️Problems and answers

I forgot my password.

It cannot be reset, because nobody but you holds the key. Use your recovery key (section 3). If you have none, restore from a .ppn backup. Otherwise the data cannot be opened by anyone, including me.

The fingerprint option does not appear, and Android asks for my phone password.

No fingerprint is saved on the phone. Open Android Settings, then Biometrics and security, then Fingerprints, and add one. The password Android asks for is your phone's screen-lock password, not a My PSWD password.

Fingerprint unlock stopped working.

Tap the link to use your recovery key or master password, unlock, and turn fingerprint unlock on again in Settings, then Security, then Lock method. Write down the new recovery key it shows. Adding a fingerprint or changing your screen lock can reset the phone's key, which is why this can happen.

I cannot take a screenshot.

That is deliberate. The app blocks screenshots to protect your passwords.

The app locked by itself.

Auto-lock closes the vault after the time you chose, and after one minute in the background. Change it in Settings, then Auto-lock.

My phone's icon looks old after an update.

Some phones keep old icons until restart. Restart the phone, or remove and re-add the icon.

Does My PSWD use the internet or the cloud?

No. It has no internet permission, and your vault never leaves your phone.

I lost my phone.

Your vault is encrypted and locked. Install My PSWD on a new phone and import your latest backup. Without a backup or recovery key, nobody can recover it.

How do I remove my data?

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then My PSWD, then Storage, then Clear data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Before I update the app.

Export a .ppn backup first. Your vault opens exactly as before after an update, but a backup is cheap insurance.

🐞Reporting a problem

To help me fix things quickly, please include in your email:

  • your phone, for example "Galaxy S26 FE" and your Android version
  • the My PSWD version (Settings, then About)
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot of the message if you can. Screenshots are blocked inside the app, so use your phone's camera or write the words down.

Never include passwords, recovery keys, your master password or backup files.

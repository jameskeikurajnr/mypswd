My PSWD for Android: Support

A private, offline vault for your passwords and secure notes. Needs Android 11 or newer.

Need help? Contact me using the email on the main Support page. Please do not send passwords, recovery keys or your master password. I will never ask for them.

Common questions

I forgot my password.
It cannot be reset, because nobody but you holds the key. If you saved a recovery key, tap "Use a recovery key or master password instead" on the lock screen and type it. Otherwise restore from an encrypted backup (.ppn) if you have one.

How do I back up my vault?
Open Settings and choose the encrypted backup option. Save the .ppn file somewhere safe. Do this before every app update.

The fingerprint option does not appear. Android asks for my phone password.
That means no fingerprint is saved on the phone. Open Android Settings, then Biometrics and security, then Fingerprints, and add one. Then reopen My PSWD and tap the scanner. The password Android asks for is your phone's screen-lock password, not a My PSWD password.

Why can I not use face unlock?
Many Android phones, including Samsung, treat face unlock as a weaker lock, and Android does not let apps use it to protect secrets. Fingerprint and your screen lock work.

Does My PSWD use the internet or the cloud?
No. The Android app has no internet permission, and your vault never leaves your phone.

I got a new phone. How do I move my vault?
On the old phone, export an encrypted backup (.ppn). Move the file to the new phone, install My PSWD, and import it. Keep your recovery key too.

I lost my phone.
Your vault is encrypted and locked. Install My PSWD on a new phone and import your latest backup. Without a backup or recovery key, nobody can recover the vault, not even me.

How do I remove my data?
Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then My PSWD, then Storage, then Clear data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

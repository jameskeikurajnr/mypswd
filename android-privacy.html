Privacy Policy: Android

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: My PSWD for Android does not collect, send or share any of your data. Your vault stays on your phone, encrypted.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

Where your data lives

Your passwords and notes are saved only on your phone, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The Android app does not have the internet permission at all, so it cannot upload your vault anywhere. I (the developer) cannot see, recover or reset your vault.

Fingerprint and screen-lock unlock

If you turn on fingerprint or screen-lock unlock, the check is done by Android's built-in system. The app never receives your fingerprint, face or screen-lock details. It only gets a yes or no. A random key is kept in the phone's secure Android Keystore, protected by your fingerprint or screen lock, and it never leaves your phone.

Permissions

  • Biometrics / fingerprint: only to unlock your vault.
  • Vibration: for small touch feedback.

The app does not ask for internet, storage, contacts, camera, microphone or location permission.

Backups and exports

If you export a backup, it is an encrypted file saved or shared where you choose. You decide where it goes. Keep it safe, and remember that a lost master password or recovery key cannot be reset by me.

Email and ratings

The Help button opens your own mail app. It only sends something if you press send in your mail app. The Rate button opens Google Play. Google handles Google Play under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes your vault from the phone. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children

My PSWD is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

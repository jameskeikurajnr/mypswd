📱What is stored on your phone

These items live only inside the app's private storage on your phone. No other app can read them.

  • Your vault: logins, secure notes, folders, categories and favourites, saved as one encrypted block.
  • Your settings: look and colour theme, language, auto-lock time, backup reminder and similar choices.
  • The name you enter (optional) so the app can greet you. It is kept inside your encrypted vault backup.
  • The Bin: items you delete stay for 30 days, still encrypted, then are removed.
  • Automatic backups: the app keeps a small rolling set of encrypted copies on the phone, so a mistake can be undone. They use the key that was current when each copy was made.
  • Break-in Log: a record, kept on the phone, of wrong-password attempts.

🔐How your vault is protected

  • Everything is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is saved.
  • If you use a master password, the encryption key is made from it with Argon2id, a slow method that makes guessing very hard. The password itself is never stored.
  • If you use fingerprint or screen-lock unlock, the app uses a long random key. That key is kept in the Android Keystore, protected by your fingerprint or screen lock, and does not leave your phone.
  • With a PIN, the real key is a long random one. After 10 wrong tries the PIN is removed and you use your recovery key.
Think of it as a safe-deposit box inside your own phone. Only you hold the key, and there is no copy at a bank.

👆Fingerprint and screen-lock unlock

If you turn this on, the check is done by Android's own system. The app never receives your fingerprint, face or screen-lock details. It only gets a yes or no. Some Android phones, including Samsung, treat face unlock as a weaker lock, so Android does not let apps use it for secrets. Fingerprint and your screen lock work.

🌐Internet and third parties

  • The app has no internet permission and no way to contact any server. It cannot upload your vault.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code and no code from data-collecting third parties.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ups or logins to a service.

📋Clipboard and screen

  • When you tap Copy next to a username or password, the copied text is cleared from the clipboard after about 20 seconds. Panic Lock also clears it.
  • The app blocks screenshots and hides its picture in the recent-apps view, so a password cannot be captured by accident. This is a security feature, not a fault.
  • The vault locks itself after a time you choose (1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes of no use) and after one minute in the background.

💾Backups, exports and import

  • Export: you can save an encrypted backup file (.ppn). It is made on your phone and goes only where you choose, for example a folder or a share option you pick. It stays encrypted.
  • Import: you can import a .ppn vault file, or notes, Word or CSV files from other places. The file is read on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Android system backup is switched off for this app, so Google backup and device-transfer tools do not copy your vault.
  • If you share or print a file, it is stored by the app or service you chose, and their rules apply to that copy.

🛡️Safety tools, and what they keep

  • Recovery key: a long code you can create so you can get back in if you forget the password. Write it on paper. I cannot see or recover it.
  • Emergency Sheet: a letter or wallet-size card that holds your recovery key for someone you trust. If you print or share it, you are responsible for it.
  • Decoy vault: an optional second password that opens a separate harmless vault. It is stored on the phone like the real one, and does not change your real vault.
  • Health Check: checks your own passwords on the phone for weak, reused, very common or old ones. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Password generator and one-time codes: created on the phone. One-time codes (2FA) are calculated on the phone from the secret you save.
  • Panic Lock: hold the lock button to lock at once and clear the clipboard.
  • Wrong-password limit: if you turn on the option to erase after repeated wrong passwords, the vault is erased on the phone after that many tries.

✉️The Help and Rate buttons

The Help button opens your own mail app with a blank message. It sends something only if you press send in your mail app. The Rate button opens the Google Play page, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

🗑️Deleting your data

  • All of your data is in the app on your phone, so you control it.
  • To delete everything, uninstall My PSWD, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then My PSWD, then Storage, then Clear data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • Because I hold no copy, I cannot access, export or delete your vault for you, and I cannot recover it if you lose your phone, backup and recovery key.

▶️Google Play

When you download My PSWD, Google handles the download under its own privacy policy. Google may give developers anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of installs or crash reports, but only if you have chosen to share them in your device's settings. This does not identify you and never includes your vault.

If you join a Google Play test of My PSWD, Google may share the email address you used for the invitation, and any feedback or crash reports you choose to send, with the developer. These are used only to improve the app.

📨Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time. Please never send passwords, recovery keys or your master password.

🧱Security

Because your information never leaves your phone, it is protected by your encrypted vault and by your phone's own security, such as your screen lock and fingerprint. We recommend keeping your phone's software up to date, keeping your recovery key on paper, and keeping a backup somewhere safe.

🎛️Your choices

  • Turn a permission off: Android Settings, then Apps, then My PSWD, then Permissions.
  • Delete an item: delete it in the app. It stays in the Bin for 30 days, then is removed.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or use Clear data (see Deleting your data above).
